SugarPop

Swap, match & pop your way through 10 sweet levels!

How to play

  • Swap two neighbouring candies (click, or drag) to line up 3 or more of the same kind.
  • Match 4 → striped candy (clears a whole row / column).
  • L or T shape → blast candy (clears a 3×3 area).
  • Match 5 → rainbow bomb (swap it with any candy to clear that colour).
  • Reach the level goals before you run out of moves!
